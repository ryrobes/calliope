# Neighborhood illustration

`neighborhood.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on
2026-10-08, then copied unchanged into this app. It is concept artwork, not an
image of any listed property. Both renderers display that distinction.

Prompt:

> Create a polished wide landscape editorial architectural illustration for a fictional real estate business workspace, 1536x1024 or wider. Scene: a quiet contemporary neighborhood with three distinct homes, layered trees and a winding path, viewed from a slightly elevated oblique angle. Mixed media architectural collage: fine precise ink lines, softly shaded geometric building forms, subtle textured paper, sophisticated sage green, deep forest teal, warm ivory and restrained terracotta accents. Architectural magazine art direction, calm premium feel, generous sky and clean negative space in upper left, detail clustered toward right and lower half. No text, no logos, no UI, no borders, no people. This is concept artwork, not photography of an actual listing. Output intended as a cropped panoramic banner in a native desktop and mobile business app.

The `CArtwork` catalog node accepts the enum `neighborhood`; each renderer maps
it to this known asset. Definitions cannot provide arbitrary URLs or file paths.
