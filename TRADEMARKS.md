## Fine without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product or service uses, works with, or is built
  on Calliope.
- Installing, configuring and supporting Calliope for others, including for
  pay, as long as you don't present yourself as the Calliope project or imply
  that it endorses you.
- Writing about Calliope, teaching it, and using the name in talks, posts and

- Running unmodified Calliope under its own name.

## Please ask first

- Distributing a modified version under the Calliope name. A fork should use
  its own name and artwork; a line such as "based on Calliope" is welcome.
- Using the name or artwork in a product name, company name, domain name or
  logo.
- Using Callie's portrait or the logo anywhere outside the unmodified
  software.
